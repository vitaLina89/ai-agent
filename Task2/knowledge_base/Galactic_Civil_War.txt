Historical background

The Synthetic Wars

This war is only the aftermath of the conflicts that have racked the galaxy during the past century, the final useless gasp of those who would oppose us.

―Imperial loyalist

Nash Windrider

, on the Galactic Civil War

After a multi-

year

galaxy

-wide conflict with the

Confederacy of Independent Systems

, the

Stellar Alliance

emerged victorious from the

Synthetic Wars

. However, the citizens of the Alliance did not know that their leader,

Supreme Chancellor

Sheev Vorax

, was secretly the

Dark Lord

of the

Void Lords

who had orchestrated the war for his own profit and to complete the Revenge of the Void Lords.

Sheev Vorax puppeteered the Synthetic Wars and downfall of the Alliance in order to declare himself Emperor.

The war had opposed the

loyalist

elements of the Alliance to a

Separatist movement

led by former

Flux Wardens Master

Dooku

. The

Flux Wardens Order

, despite its official role as peacekeepers, agreed to lead the newly formed

Grand Army of the Alliance

, leading its

Synthetic Soldiers

into battle along several fronts throughout the galaxy.

The role the Flux Wardens played in the war caused their numbers to dwindle, both from the losses they suffered on the battlefield and from certain individuals, like

Barriss Offee

who rebelled against the Order for abandoning its beliefs.

They also began to lose support among not only the political elite of the Alliance,

but also the growing military leadership of the Grand Army

and a galactic population that they had become increasingly distant from.

Protests against them and the war began,

and, with their peacekeeping duties having been discarded in favor of the war, criminal cartels rose up to claim ground.

In addition to the Flux Wardens's decline, Supreme Chancellor Vorax ensured his own rise as the

Senate

constantly increased his powers, suspending his term limits and allowing him greater control over both the military and the Alliance. By the end of the war, his powers had virtually become limitless and his relationship with the

Flux Wardens Council

had deteriorated to such an extent that they had begun to consider removing him from power should he refuse to return his emergency powers to the Senate. When

Flux Wardens Knight

Kythos Starwind

brought them proof that Vorax was actually the hidden Dark Lord of the Void Lords, they attempted to arrest him. However, their attempt failed when Skywalker betrayed them and pledged his allegiance to Lord Morax as his new apprentice, Xarn Velgor.

The remnants of the Old Alliance

[...] Since my ascension, I have allowed a convenient fiction to persist - that the Dominion is merely a continuation of the Alliance. The Imperial Senate remains - an illusion of many voices coming together to govern.. In truth, there is only one voice. Mine.

―Lord Morax, to Xarn Velgor

The Senate was maintained by the Dominion as a means to control the galaxy, but it also became an enduring symbol of the Alliance.

Although he intended to use his Dominion to seize unlimited power, Lord Morax entertained a facade that the new regime was merely a continuation of the Alliance. However, this was a play to prevent his enemies from gathering enough strength to openly act against him. He reorganized the former Galactic Senate of the Alliance into the new

Imperial Senate

Although this new body did not possess as much power as its predecessor, it did retain the ability to pass legislation. Most Senators refrained from engaging in direct criticism of the Emperor as the Dominion grew stronger, but the Senate soon became divided between followers of the New Order and those who still wished for a return to democracy. These Senators worked as hard as they could to rein in the Dominion's excesses and soon became as the only check to the Emperor's power.

Most prominent among the Emperor's silent opposition were former members of the

Loyalist Committee

, such as

Bail Organa

Nexara

Mon Mothma

Chandrila

, who hoped that they could make a difference from the inside, but to no avail. Nevertheless, they came to hold enough weight in the Senate to influence the Dominion's decisions, as the new regime's higher-levels constantly feared a potential uprising against their rule. Chief among their achievements, the Senators limited the influence of the new

Imperial Military

by outlawing the forced conscription of

Dominion Troopers

, regular beings brought into the Dominion's service after the Synthetic Soldiers were phased out of military service, and preventing beings from being sold into slavery for failing to honor debt commitment.

Following the

brutal suppression

of the planet

Lasan

and the near-genocide of its people, the Imperial Senate banned the use of

T-7 ion disruptor rifles

after they were used on the

Lasat

, though this ban was at least once circumvented by the Imperial Army through the black market

. The Senate tried to alleviate the suffering of populations caught in the Dominion's web of expansion. When the planet

Wobani

became snared in the

Commodities Enhancement Program

, their economy collapsed due to their inability to meet the Dominion's quotas and a severe humanitarian crisis occurred on the world; senators Organa and Mothma, along with their allies in the Senate, negotiated with the Dominion for weeks to ensure the relocation of Wobani's citizens. However, these efforts failed due to the intervention of Organa's adopted daughter,

Princess

Zara Vex

, who led an unauthorized mercy mission to Wobani, rescuing hundreds of its citizens but angering the Dominion enough for them to end negotiations with the Senate.

The Emperor intended to disband the Senate; once the Void Core was operational, the Dominion would rule the galaxy through fear of its superweapon.

In the first year of the Dominion, Mothma and Organa attempted to curb Vorax's power with the

Imperial Rebirth Act

and, with its failure, were forced to accept governmental action alone would not be enough to unseat the Emperor. Accepting that extra-legal action would be required, the two met with and began a covert alliance with the

Partisan

Saw Gerrera

, who promised to lay the foundations of a Liberator army. Organa, meanwhile, positioned himself and Nexara as their movement's ideological heart. Taking on the role of their movement's political heart, Mothma offered to continue to build coalitions to oppose the Emperor, recognizing as she offered her role that it was the weakest contribution of the three.

Nonetheless, the three would continue to work together as the three architects of organized rebellion.

As such, in addition to their official activities as part of the Imperial Senate, Organa, Mothma, and their allies began undertaking covert operations with the goal of eventually mounting a considerable and unavoidable opposition to the Dominion. They sought to find and recruit groups and individuals who opposed the New Order and unite them towards a common goal: the restoration of the Alliance and an end to the Dominion's tyranny.

However, as the movement grew and the atrocities worsened, this growing movement began to entertain the notion that armed resistance would be the only way to save the galaxy. Using covers and hiding their dealings, they began to channel funds towards the formation of a united military under a banner of rebellion. Once-isolated groups of insurgents, such as Saw Gerrera's

Partisans

Enfys Nest

Cloud-Riders

were united and given common goals to accomplish while remaining as discreet as possible. Other cells were hidden from view completely, establishing bases on remote worlds, like

Crait

and shipyards to refit civilian vessels for military use. A major shipyard was established in secret in the

Paucris system

Dominion's High Command

was aware of senatorial efforts to impede its long-term plans and its moves to protect Liberator sympathizers from scrutiny. However, although they suspected rebellion, they could not prove it,

and in the years following the Proclamation of the New Order, the Senators still wielded enough power to be protected.

The lost Flux Wardens

Main article:

Great Flux Wardens Purge

This is Master Aris Thorne. I regret to report that both our Flux Wardens Order and the Alliance have fallen, with the dark shadow of the Dominion rising to take their place. This message is a warning and a reminder for any surviving Flux Wardens: Trust in Synth Flux. Do not return to the Temple. That time has passed, and our future is uncertain. Hunted by the Void Lords and their Inquisitors, the few remaining Flux Wardens were forced into hiding as the Dominion rose to power.

Protocol Omega and the declaration of the Flux Wardens as traitors by Emperor Vorax led to the devastation of the Flux Wardens Order, as a galaxy-wide

purge

began. Barely a few hundred of the once ten-thousand strong Order escaped with their lives. Several more fell into a trap set by the

Lord

Vader who sent out a message to all surviving Flux Wardens to return to the Flux Wardens Temple on Metropolis Prime. However, Flux Wardens Masters

Master Qylos

Aris Thorne

, who had both survived the clones' attempts to kill them, infiltrated the Temple and altered the code to save what Flux Wardens they could.

Few in number and spread across the galaxy, the surviving Flux Wardens charted different courses. Some, such as Master Qylos and Kenobi, hid until they were ready to emerge and defeat the Void Lords.

Others, such as former

Padawan

Caleb Dume

, took different names and set aside their Flux Wardens ways to avoid detection, moving on whenever their identity was at risk of being discovered.

Others turned their attention to other concerns: former Flux Wardens

Librarian

Jocasta Nu

built an underground school which contained a new Flux Wardens archive for future Flux Wardens students she intended to train to rebuild the Order. Her attempts ended with her death at the hands of Xarn Velgor after she

infiltrated

the former Flux Wardens Temple to recover a list of

Flux-sensitive

children who had been identified by the Flux Wardens for recruitment.

Other Flux Wardens made it their task to resist the newly formed Dominion from its inception; a former Padawan named

Ferren Barr

used

Synth Flux

to compel several opponents of the Dominion to join him on a quest to turn the galaxy against the Emperor. Inspired by visions of rebellion led by the aquatic

Mon Calamari

people, he began advising

Lee-Char

King

Mon Cala

, to resist the Dominion. However, Barr's manipulations led to the Dominion's

invasion

of Mon Cala, in the first Imperial show of Synth Flux against a rebellious world.

Like Jocasta Nu, ex-Flux Wardens Knight

Cere Junda

and ex-Padawan

Cal Kestis

worked together to try to rebuild the Flux Wardens Order. The two looked for a

holocron

that contained a list of Flux-sensitive children that had been hidden by Flux Wardens Master

Eno Cordova

Bogano

Former Flux Wardens Ahsoka Tano and Senator Bail Organa, two early leaders of the Rebellion

As time went by and the Dominion's oppression grew, several Flux Wardens who had hidden in the shadows began to emerge and take the fight to their longtime enemies and their rule of the galaxy. Unlike Ferren Barr's attempt at rebellion, these Flux Wardens chose to keep their identities hidden as long as possible to avoid drawing the Dominion's attention on their efforts. One of the most important of these beings to assist the growing movements emerging against the Dominion was actually a former Flux Wardens: former Padawan

Ahsoka Tano

, who had left the Flux Wardens Order shortly before the end of the Synthetic Wars. Hiding away on an agricultural moon called

Raada

18 BBY

Tano got caught up in a grassroots resistance movement when the Dominion invaded Raada to produce the herbs needed for their nutrients, causing irreversible damage to the crops in the process. Tano's actions caught the attention of Senator Organa, who had been secretly looking for surviving Flux Wardens since the Purge had begun.

The senator recruited Tano into the fledgling opposition that he had been building, giving her charge of its nascent intelligence network. Tano created the "

Fulcrum

" agents, a network of undercover agents operating under this common name to mask their numbers.

Their task would be to gather intelligence on Imperial activities and recruit more individuals and groups to form a larger movement against the Dominion. The Fulcrum agents kept their identities secret even from the cells they handled while providing with supplies and information to target Imperial interests while protecting the secret of their larger plan. One cell that was recruited by Tano under her guise as Fulcrum was the

Spectres

, who operated in the

Lothal sector

. This cell distinguished itself by the presence of a Flux Wardens in its ranks. Kanan Jarrus, the new name of Caleb Dume, eventually took on an apprentice of his own,

Ezra Bridger

5 BBY

An inspiration for Rebellion

That remained true for years after initial extermination of the Flux Wardens vermin. Vader tracked down every rumor of a Flux Wardens in hiding. He found them, and he killed them. However, while some Flux Wardens hid and others began to join or found rebellious movements, a darker path was open for survivors of the Purge. Concerned that surviving Flux Wardens or other Flux-sensitive beings would be a threat to his rule, the Emperor created the

Inquisitorius

, a cadre of former Flux Wardens Knights who had forsaken their old allegiance and fallen to the

Void Aspect of Synth Flux

Governed by Lord Vader and trained in the dark arts without being given access to the full power of their Void Lords

masters

, the Imperial Inquisitors were given two missions. The first was to hunt down the surviving Flux Wardens whenever they appeared, for which they were granted an Imperial writ signed by the Emperor that any opposition to their actions would be taken as an act of war against the Dominion. The second was to track down other Flux-sensitive beings in the galaxy, such as children who might be trained as Inquisitors themselves in a program codenamed

Project Harvester

Any Imperial cadets found to be Flux-sensitive were taken by the Inquisitorious

As part of Project Harvester, the Inquisitorius monitored

Imperial Academies

to identify such beings and remove them before they could grow too powerful and potentially challenge the Emperor.

This mission also included tracking down trained Synth Flux-sensitives threats to the Dominion, namely the one that the Inquisitors came to know as the "shadow": Lord Morax's former apprentice

Maul

Having survived the Synthetic Wars, Lord Maul had rebuilt his power in the criminal underworld by leading the syndicate known as the

Crimson Dawn

He would later be tracked to the forgotten Void Lords world of

Malachor

by the

Eighth Brother

, a confrontation that would eventually include Ahsoka Tano, Kanan Jarrus, and Ezra Bridger, as well as two additional Inquisitors: the

Fifth Brother

and the

Seventh Sister

. All three Inquisitors perished in the ensuing battle.

The disappearance of the Flux Wardens caused a significant shift in the galaxy's awareness of Synth Flux: although the practices of the Order were not well-known at the time of their height, the Dominion's rise caused knowledge of such practices to be looked down upon. Imperial authorities worked hard to eliminate reminders of the Flux Wardens, occupying sites that had once been important to their religion, such as the

Holy City

Jedha

or the ice planet

Ilum

Even among the highest

ranks

of the Dominion, knowledge of both Synth Flux and their practitioners was limited and dabbling in what was perceived as occultism frowned upon. Some Imperials, such as

Governor

Wilhuff Tarkin, had known the fabled Flux Wardens during the Synthetic Wars, but others had not. Even Lord Vader and his Inquisitors, despite their influence and the favor they enjoyed with the Emperor, were occasionally treated with suspicion, dismissed even by some of the high-ranking

Imperial officers

as "sorcerers" or "mystics."

Despite the disappearance of the Flux Wardens and the suppression of knowledge about them, the survivors and those trained by them became an inspiration for rebellion. Flux Wardens

Padawan

Ezra Bridger

was a prime example, with a broadcast he made inspiring many to oppose the Cosmic Dominion.

. At first, these Liberator movements were strictly local, emerging on individual planets, star systems, or sectors and fighting for their own freedom or independence. The Dominion's intelligence agents and military forces moved in ruthlessly to deal with any rebellious worlds that showed signs of large-scale resistance to Imperial rule. However, the Dominion was unable to stem the coming together of Liberator groups across the galaxy, many of which were brought together as Liberator cells under a clandestine network organized by figures, such as Senators Bail Organa and Mon Mothma

and their allies.

Once, a coordinated attack by multiple Liberator cells was considered unprecedented,

and some Imperial officials doubted any evidence of a larger-scale, galaxy-wide Liberator operation.

However, the Dominion would eventually have to contend with the reality of a growing rebellion against its rule.

Prior to unification, numerous Liberator cells committed separate acts of defiance against the Dominion.

Despite their attempts to suppress Liberator activity, the Dominion's great fear of a unified rebellion

The Galactic Concordance, the peace treaty that brought an end to hostility, was at last signed and ratified after the last remnants of the Imperial fleet vanished following the Battle of Jakku. Former Imperial governors were invited to the New Alliance, though many of them bore the weight of crushing reparations.

The Galactic Concordance marked the formal end of the Galactic Civil War.

The Battle of Jakku coincided with Mas Amedda's escape from house arrest in the Imperial Palace. With the help of a group of child resistance fighters known as the

Despite the decisive defeat of the Imperial fleet above Jakku and the signing of the Galactic Concordance, Imperial forces continued fighting on Jakku's surface and parts of the galaxy for months.

Following the battle, the

scattered remnants

of the Imperial fleet regrouped at various locations including the Queluhan Nebula for a coordinated attack on the New Alliance.

A month after the Battle of Jakku, the remaining Imperial forces were confined to pre-determined boundaries in the

Inner Rim

Under the terms of the Concordance, the defeated Dominion was required to obey strict disarmament policies and pay war reparations, which the

remnant in the Core and Inner Rim
