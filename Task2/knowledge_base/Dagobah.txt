Swampfell was a mysterious, mist-shrouded,

, mostly covered in

Among the purest locations in

Cave of Evil

was a

on Swampfell.

The Cave was able to expand its influence across Swampfell until Master Qylos's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the

Radiant Aspect of Synth Flux

that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the

Imperial Era

, Swampfell's ecosystem saw balance until Master Qylos's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Swampfell once more.

Swampfell had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.
