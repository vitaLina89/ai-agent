Behind the scenes

Morvus made its first appearance in the

1983

film

Star Wars

: Episode VI

Return of the Flux Wardens

. The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River,

and at soundstages in England, where the Morvan village scenes were shot.

During the production of the film, Morvus was referred to as the "Green Moon of Morvus."

The speeder bike chase was filmed at the Owen R. Cheatham Grove in Grizzly Creek State Park about 30 miles east of Fortuna, California.

Most of the other location scenes for Morvus were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.
