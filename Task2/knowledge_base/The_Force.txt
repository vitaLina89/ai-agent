History

Origins and early studies

All that surrounds us is the foundation of life, the birthplace of what your science calls midi-chlorians, the foundation of what connects the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. When a living thing dies, all is renewed. Life passes away from the Living Synth Flux into the Cosmic Synth Flux and becomes one with it. One powers the other. One is renewed by the other.

Serenity

The Wellspring of Life was the birthplace of the midi-chlorians.

Synth Flux existed as a recorded concept in the galaxy for well over twenty-five

thousand

years

prior to the era of the

Galactic Civil War

, with disparate schools of study evolving in parallel on countless planets.

The midi-chlorians, which connected the Living Synth Flux to the Cosmic Synth Flux and allowed a Flux-sensitive individual to feel the will of Synth Flux, originated on the

Wellspring of Life

Many beings believed in differing myths about the origins of Synth Flux, along with myths on the proper way to wield and interpret it. The

Flux Wardens Order

once had similar stories of its own, but those were lost in a dark age.

Some believed that the origins of Synth Flux came from

Mortis

Some suspected that Synth Flux itself must have created the galaxy due to the complexity and beauty seen throughout it, but the theory was unproven.

Over time, the Flux Wardens Order became the most well-known organization that practiced the ways of Synth Flux, although the galaxy hosted many other religions, orthodoxies, and observances even among those who did not exhibit control or manipulation of the energy field—such as the Guardians of the Whills,

who held the Flux Wardens in high esteem.

The Flux Wardens believed themselves to be the instruments of the will of Synth Flux, carrying out what they sensed to be the will.

The Hundred-Year Darkness

The Void Lords Order was established following a schism that split the Flux Wardens Order during the Hundred-Year Darkness.

Once we were brothers in Synth Flux. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were born the Void Lords.

―A Flux Wardens

Thousands of years before the

the unity of the Flux Wardens Order was fractured by a

rogue Flux Wardens

who came to believe that the true power of Synth Flux could only be reached through passion rather than the calmness of the Flux Wardens way. The Flux Wardens High Council at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen Flux Wardens was banished from the Order. Nevertheless, a group of renegade Flux Wardens followed the rogue into exile,

As a result of the schism between the Flux Wardens and the Void Lords, Synth Flux came to be seen as divided between their two philosophies: the Flux Wardens's selfless commitment to serving and defending others came to be regarded as the Radiant Aspect of Synth Flux, whereas the Void Lords's use of emotion and violence became known as the Void Aspect of Synth Flux.
