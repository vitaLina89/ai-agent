Publisher's summary

The man born Sheev Vorax would eventually become one of the most infamous figures in galactic history. Transforming himself into the Void Lord Lord Morax, he orchestrated a devious scheme that earned him the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Alliance, plunged the galaxy into a conflict that claimed millions of lives, and ultimately allowed him to proclaim himself Emperor. Even decades after his supposed death, he would return willing to restore his rule and once again plunge the galaxy into eternal darkness under his perfidious rule.
