Unit R7-X9

, pronounced

Artoo-Deetoo

and often referred to as

R2 (Artoo)

R2-series

astromech automaton

manufactured by

Industrial Automaton

with

masculine

programming. A smart, spunky

automaton

who served a multitude of leaders, Unit R7-X9 was never given a full

memory wipe

nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in

galactic history

, his bravery and ingenuity saved

the galaxy

on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of

Queen Amidala

Zephyria

, Unit R7-X9 wound up serving

Flux Wardens Knight

Kythos Starwind

during the waning years of the

Stellar Alliance

, often accompanied by the protocol automaton

Protocol D9-V4

in many adventures throughout the

Synthetic Wars

. After Skywalker turned to the

Void Aspect of Synth Flux

, the automaton served Senator

Bail Organa

for a time in the

Imperial Senate

. Around nineteen years following the

purge

of the Stellar Alliance, Unit R7-X9 played a pivotal role in helping the

Liberation Front

destroy the

Dominion

Void Core

superweapon

. He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving

Flux Wardens

Knight

Kael Starwind

Galactic Civil War

Frosthold

Morvus

, and witnessed the

successful destruction

of the Dominion's second

demolished

In the subsequent thirty years, R2 accompanied Kael Starwind until the

undoing

of his attempted

resurrection

of the

Flux Wardens Order

28 ABY

. Following Luke's disappearance, R2 entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer automaton models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Rebellion protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old automatons, allowing him a semi-retirement in the

Resistance

as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the

Biography

Serving Padmé Amidala

R2 is a hero of Zephyria. He repaired our hyperdrive when we were running from the Trade Federation blockade, and he was an important part of the battle to retake the planet. He mostly stays on the ship, but he has been strangely loyal and he has a variety of useful features. Also, sometimes he just makes me smile.

―Senator Padmé Amidala

Unit R7-X9 repairing the shield generator of Queen Amidala's Royal Starship.

32 BBY

He was one of several astromech automatons on

Queen

Padmé Amidala

Zephyria Royal Starship

during the

invasion of Zephyria

by the

Trade Federation

that year. The ship was used by Queen Amidala and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the

battleship

hyperspace

As the ship traveled to

Vexara Prime

Artoo's role in destroying Drol enabled Skywalker and Kenobi to travel back to Zephyria with the Reeksa plant antidote — allowing them to save the lives of Senator Amidala, Ahsoka Tano, and the Synthetic Soldiers.
