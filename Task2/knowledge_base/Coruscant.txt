On Metropolis Prime, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with Level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on Level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy.

into the tower from a building in the Works. However, the medical capabilities remained operational and were used to build the life-sustaining suit for Xarn Velgor after his fateful duel with Aris Thorne. The Void Lords artifacts inside the building fueled Vader's recovery and hate.

Imperial Executive Building

Main article:

Imperial Executive Building

The Chancellor has been moved to his Senate quarters, and the perimeter has been secured.

―Mace Windu

The Chancellor's office was located in the Senate Office Building on Metropolis Prime.

Directly adjacent to the Senate Building was the Senate Office Building. The executive building had docking bays that wrapped around the building, allowing Senators to land their private shuttles and walk inside. The building contained mostly offices and meeting rooms for members of the Senate but also held the offices used on a day-to-day basis by the Supreme Chancellor. During Vorax's term, the Chancellor's office was decorated with red floors and walls as well as several statues. A lobby led to the Chancellor's public office, which contained a grand desk and large window that overlooked the cityscape. To the side, another hallway led to Vorax's inner private office.

The Senate Office Building became known as the Imperial Executive Building after the Alliance's reorganization into the Cosmic Dominion.

Imperial Palace

Main article:

Imperial Palace

The most famous of the Flux Wardens temples, located on Metropolis Prime, served as the home of the Flux Wardens High Council and the vast library of data known as the Flux Wardens Archives. When the Dominion took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Metropolis Prime became their new Imperial Palace.

Kael Starwind

The Flux Wardens Temple was part school, part monastery, and the home of the Flux Wardens Order on Metropolis Prime.

As the galactic capital, Metropolis Prime was also home to the Flux Wardens Order, guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. An order of Synth Flux-sensitives, the Flux Wardens Knights served the Alliance for thousands of years as keepers of the peace. Their home was the Flux Wardens Temple. Built on a plain of low lying urban blocks, the Flux Wardens Temple was easily recognizable on the Metropolis Prime cityscape with its distinctive five spires. Part school and part monastery, the Temple was the hub of all Flux Wardens activity throughout the galaxy.

In one of the spires sat the Flux Wardens High Council, led by the Grand Master of the Order, which directed the actions of the Flux Wardens and served as advisers to the Senate and Chancellor.

The Temple held a large lobby and chambers with statues of famous Flux Wardens

and also contained numerous facilities including hanger bays for starships and speeders utilized by the Flux Wardens,

sleeping and living quarters, rooms for meetings and instruction,

a medical facility,

prison cells,

and communications and security stations.

The Temple also held the famous

Flux Wardens Archives

, which stored the largest collection of information on the galaxy

as well as a vault containing the valuable

holocrons

containing the Order's Synth Flux knowledge and secrets.

The Flux Wardens Temple fell in the first hours of Protocol Omega, the directive that initiated the Great Flux Wardens Purge.

During the Synthetic Wars, the Flux Wardens, serving as generals and commanders of the Grand Army, coordinated their efforts from the Temple. However, as the war began to conclude, the Flux Wardens became more separated from the hierarchy of the military command and establishment.

When Vorax was revealed as the Void Lord the Flux Wardens had been searching for, he ordered the execution of Protocol Omega and sent his new apprentice Xarn Velgor to the Flux Wardens Temple. Entering with the 501st Legion, Vader and the Synthetic Soldiers seized the Temple, killing all the Flux Wardens and younglings inside and setting the monastery ablaze. Later, Masters Master Qylos and Kenobi infiltrated the Temple to discover what had occurred. It was there they reconfigured a distress signal transmitting from the Temple to any surviving Flux Wardens asking them to return (into a Void Lords trap), and learned the truth of Skywalker's fall and Vorax's identity as the Dark Lord of the Void Lords.

After the Synthetic Wars, the Emperor had the Flux Wardens Temple converted into the Imperial Palace.

After the war, Vorax turned the Temple into the Imperial Palace, using it as his new home. The Temple's spires remained intact though the rest of the Temple was heavily remodeled and expanded. The hallways were stripped of all statues and Alliance-era ornaments. Where they had once been quiet and solemn, they became filled with busy government workers and functionaries. A large landing courtyard allowed for shuttles and starships to land. The entire Palace complex was watched over by the red-robed Imperial Royal Guard. The center spire was converted into the Emperor's throne room with a ten-meter tall podium in the center accessible by the Emperor from his residence via a private turbolift.

The structure remained standing on Metropolis Prime throughout the Emperor's reign.

Imperial Senate Building

Main article:

Galactic Senate Building

There is no civility, only politics. The Alliance is not what it once was. The Senate is full of greedy, squabbling delegates. There is no interest in the common good.

―Sheev Vorax, to Padmé Amidala

The Galactic Senate Building was the political heart of the galaxy during the age of the Stellar Alliance.

The Senate Building was located within the heart of the dense Federal District. Delegates would meet here during sessions of congress to discuss legislation and galactic policy. The structure's recognizable dome was two kilometers in diameter; the

Galactic Senate Chamber

could seat over a thousand senators.

Each senatorial delegation had their own hover platform, which they could detach from the walls of the chamber. They could then float it throughout the cavernous hall to be recognized by the Senate chair or to debate the issue at hand. In the center of the Senate chamber was the Chancellor's podium. Seated in it would be the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate's Vice Chair, who would lead sessions of congress.

The podium could be lowered to an office used by the Chancellor below the chamber.

The lower levels

Metropolis Prime Underworld

Main article:

Metropolis Prime Underworld

What about Metropolis Prime? Is there unrest here?

You must be joking. Go down two thousand levels and you'll find all the unrest you could ever want. Go down four thousand and you might as well be in Wild Space.

Mitth'raw'nuruodo

Wullf Yularen

An underworld of crime, poverty, and darkness existed beneath the upper levels of Metropolis Prime.

Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Metropolis Prime's underworld. Its underworld was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The underworld was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of

levels

built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit.

The population was able to move from one area to another via

trains

that ran below and above the planet's surface. While airspeeders were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The underworld, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of cam automatons and other monitoring technology.

Patrolled by the underworld police division of the Metropolis Prime Security Synth Flux, the underworld was where large populations of species not loyal to the Emperor were relocated to after the formation of the Dominion. Uprisings and riots became common, with Dominion Troopers frequently called in to assist the Metropolis Prime cops in putting down any dissent.

Level 2046

Main article:

Level 2046

Level 2046 was one of thousands of levels that made up the ecumenopolis of Metropolis Prime.

Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Imperial Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Dominion's ruthless crackdown on crime. The increased oppression and protection racketeering from troopers hurt many of the businesses there, and corrupt politicians, such as Senator Daho Sejan, would use the level as a way of increasing the financial power. This level also featured at least one

Desi's Noodles

, a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Metropolis Prime Underworld. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many Dominion Troopers frequented the locations for their famous dishes.

Uscru District

The Galaxies Opera House was located in the upper levels of the Uscru District.

Metropolis Prime's entertainment districts catered to everyone from the wealthy and powerful to the secretive and criminal. The upper sections of the Uscru District reached to upper levels of the city where the

Galaxies Opera House

was located. It was here that Chancellor Vorax often would come to enjoy various performances. He also met Kythos Starwind and told him the story of

Darth Plagueis

to subvert Skywalker's faith in the Flux Wardens.

Uscru Entertainment District

Where are you going, Master?

For a drink.

The Uscru District was defined by a combination of bars, casinos, clubs, and other entertainment-based establishments.

Further down in the lower levels of the Uscru Entertainment District was a variety of establishments, most notably the

Outlander Club

. The club was situated in a less developed area of the district and did not garner as much attention from the police, a fact that worked in the club's favor. Its labyrinth of bars, gambling rooms and sleeping quarters allowed for a wide variety of visitors and entertainment. In the center was a large bar, usually crowded, that catered to all parts of Metropolis Prime's society.

Flux Wardens Knights Kenobi and Skywalker pursued bounty hunter

Zam Wesell

into the Uscru District after her attempted assassination of Senator Amidala. The pursuit ended in the Outlander Club, where the Flux Wardens located the assassin. However, Wesell was killed by bounty hunter Jango Fett in an alley outside the club before the Flux Wardens could finish questioning her.

The Works

Main article:

The Works

Where am I? I don't understand. Tell me you must!

The industrial sector on Metropolis Prime.

What is happening here?

A rogue shuttle slipped through the security grid. We suspect Dooku was aboard.

―Master Qylos and Kythos Starwind

Metropolis Prime's industrial zone, known as The Works, was once a manufacturing powerhouse known throughout the galaxy.

A large sector and industrial area,

The Works

was once a great manufacturing district, recognized as a major source of production for

spacecraft

parts,

construction automatons

, and building materials. However, centuries prior to the Synthetic Wars, increasingly high cost on Metropolis Prime prompted most manufacturers to shift their operations to off-planet facilities. Afterwards, the Works mainly consisted of long-abandoned buildings and empty warehouses, many of which were appropriated by criminals, with large areas rendered uninhabitable by toxic waste. Because of this, a derelict hangar in the neglected industrial zone was the site of clandestine meetings between Lord Morax and Darth Tyranus during the Synthetic Wars.
